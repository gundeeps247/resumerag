Machine Learning Engineer (New Graduate) - Northwind Analytics
Location: Hybrid (2 days in office) | Full-time | Demo job description (fictional)

ABOUT THE ROLE
Northwind Analytics builds forecasting and decision-intelligence products for retail and logistics companies. As a Machine Learning Engineer on the Customer Intelligence team, you will take models from prototype to production and help our customers understand why their customers leave, buy and return.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design, train and evaluate machine learning models for demand forecasting and customer analytics, including churn and lifetime-value models.
- Deploy models to production and monitor their performance, data quality and data drift.
- Build reliable data pipelines and features in collaboration with the data engineering team.
- Design and analyse A/B tests and communicate results clearly to product managers and customers.
- Contribute to our LLM-powered analyst assistant, which uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over customer documentation.

REQUIREMENTS
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics or a related field.
- Strong programming skills in Python and SQL.
- Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals: supervised learning, evaluation metrics, overfitting, and class imbalance.
- Hands-on experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow, and with scikit-learn.
- Experience deploying models behind REST APIs and packaging services with Docker.
- Familiarity with at least one cloud platform (AWS or GCP).
- Clear written and verbal communication.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with Kubernetes and MLOps tooling such as MLflow or Kubeflow.
- Experience with time-series forecasting.
- Experience building semantic search or retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems.
- Experience with Apache Spark or other big-data tools.
- Experience running experiments on an A/B testing platform.

WHAT WE OFFER
- Mentorship from senior ML engineers and a dedicated learning budget.
- Ownership of real production models from your first quarter.
- Competitive salary, health insurance and flexible hours.
